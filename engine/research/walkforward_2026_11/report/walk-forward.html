Rules of the two

    How the test was run

    Every position is decided at the daily close (00:00 UTC) from finished days only and held for the next day. Each change of position pays Delta India's taker fee (0.05% + 18% GST) plus slippage of 2 bp (BTC, ETH), 4 bp (SOL, XRP, DOGE, BNB) or 5 bp (the other ten), and every open position pays or earns that day's real funding. Indicators warm up on July 2024 to December 2025.

    Chosen = a profit over January–June, profit in at least 4 of the 6 months, at least 6 entries and a worst drawdown of at most 25%. These rules, the windows and every strategy were written into the code and fingerprinted before the first run; each finalist was rebuilt on data cut at 1 September with identical results before the cut.
